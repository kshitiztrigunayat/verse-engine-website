Local modifications: ORM and AO textures were resized to a maximum dimension
of 1024 with Lanczos resampling. The original 2048-pixel face/base-color image,
256-pixel normal image and logo images remain byte-identical. Palette PNGs
retain their original color palette. All images remain PNG. Geometry was subsequently reordered and
quantized with glTF Transform 4.2.1 meshopt({level: 'high'}) and meshoptimizer
0.22.0. The resulting GLB requires EXT_meshopt_compression and
KHR_mesh_quantization. No topology simplification was applied. Material
variants, glass transmission, textures, animation structure and copyright are
preserved. No endorsement implied.
