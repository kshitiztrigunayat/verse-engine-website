# Verse Engine 2.7: what's new for you

Verse Engine 2.7 is live. The [changelog](../changelog.md) lists every
change; this is the short version, written for the people who send links
to clients rather than the people who build the engine. Eight things are
new. Each one has a short guide, linked below.

## Realistic lighting, automatically

Until now a model looked its best when a designer had "baked" the lighting
into it first, in Blender or another 3D tool. If you upload a raw model,
the upload card now shows a **Lighting** choice. Pick **Bake for me**
(preview or final; **Keep as exported** is the default) and the cloud adds soft shadows and light before the link is ready. No extra
software, no second upload. Guide: [Bake for me](../guides/bake-for-me.md).

## Photo-quality stills

Stand still in any scene and, within a second or two, the view turns into
a photo-quality image: soft reflections, light bouncing off walls, glass
that looks like glass. Move and it goes back to the fast, walkable view.
You can also change every lamp and the sun's position right from the link.
Guide: [Tune quality](../guides/tune-quality.md).

## Open BIM files

Architects and developers can upload an IFC or USD file straight from
their BIM tool. Every wall, door and fixture stays its own object: click one
to see its details, hide or isolate it, or search the model floor by floor.
Guide: [Publish from IFC](../guides/publish-from-ifc.md).

## See it in your room

A chair, a lamp, a product: anything at household scale opens in phone AR
with one tap, on Android and iPhone, with no app to install. The same link
works on a laptop as a normal 3D view. Guide: [View in AR](../guides/view-in-ar.md).

## Present together

Open a scene with your client and talk inside it. One person presents and
everyone follows their view; you can point things out, take questions, and
hand over to a colleague mid-tour. Guide: [Present a scene](../guides/present-a-scene.md).

## Options and prices

Let clients switch finishes, fabrics and variants inside the link, with SKUs
and prices shown as they choose. Every choice can be sent to your own
system, so an order or a quote starts from what the client actually picked.
Guide: [Build a configurator](../guides/build-a-configurator.md).

## Screens and sound

TVs and screens in a scene can play video, and sound comes from where it
should: a television in the living room is louder in the living room.
Guide: [Video and audio](../guides/video-and-audio.md).

## Outdoors

Exteriors get a real sky that lights the scene, rain or snow, fog, and
ground you can walk on. Good for a township, a villa plot or a storefront
seen from the street. Guide: [Exteriors](../guides/exteriors.md).

## And on every device

Behind all of this is a built-in autopilot that adjusts sharpness and
effects many times a second, so the same link stays smooth from a budget
Android phone to a high-end laptop, and eases off before a phone gets hot.
The link opens in seconds; the first picture is a soft version of the whole
space, which then sharpens wherever you look.

## If you were already using Verse

Nothing you have shared changes. Existing links keep working and keep their
look. New uploads get the Lighting choice on the upload card; everything
else is on by default or a tick away in the viewer.

Try it at the [app](/app), or read the [five-minute guide](../getting-started.md).
