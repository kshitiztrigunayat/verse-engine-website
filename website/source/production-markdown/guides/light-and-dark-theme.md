# Light and dark theme

Verse follows your device by default: a light interface when your system is light, dark when it is dark.

## Change it

Use the sun/moon button in the header (on pages without a header it sits in the corner), or open the account
menu and choose **System**, **Light** or **Dark**. **System** follows your device and changes with it.

Your choice is remembered in that browser only, so it applies to every Verse page (landing, pricing, Studio,
docs) but not to other devices. The page paints in the right theme from the first frame, with no flash.

## What it does not change

The 3D scene itself is never recoloured; only the interface around it. A scene's own background (`?bg=`) and
look are unaffected.
