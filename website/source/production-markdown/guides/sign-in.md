# Sign in with a one-time code

Verse signs you in with a six-digit code sent to your email, or to your
phone on WhatsApp. There is no password to remember or reset.

## Sign in

1. Open [verse.ctruh.com/signin](https://verse.ctruh.com/signin) (or tap **Sign in** anywhere on the site).
2. Choose **Email me a code** or **WhatsApp me a code**.
3. Type your email address, or your mobile number (India, +91, is the default; for other countries pick your country first).
4. Enter the six-digit code. It works for 10 minutes and for five tries.

If this is the first time we see that address or number, a free account is created for you
(with the welcome credits and the 14-day trial with Pro features that new accounts get). If an account already
has that verified email or number, you are signed in to it.

You are brought back to what you were doing: after choosing a plan on the
[pricing page](#/guides/plans-and-pricing), saving an upload to your account, or opening
your library.

## Where you stay signed in

A sign-in lasts 30 days on that browser. **Account → Sign-in & security** lists every place you are
signed in. Sign one out, or choose **Sign out everywhere**. The same page lets you add a second way to
sign in (an email or a mobile number) to an existing account.

## Things worth knowing

- Codes are single-use. Asking for a new one cancels the old one, and a few requests per hour are allowed per address.
- If you do not get a WhatsApp code, check the number and country, and that WhatsApp is installed on that phone. Email is always an alternative.
- Plug-ins and the API do not use codes. They keep using an **access token** from Studio, and the sign-in page has a small "Use an access token" link for people who already have one. See the [API reference](#/api).

Trouble? Write to hello@ctruh.com with the address you used (never the code).
