subscribe. The [pricing page](https://verse.ctruh.com/pricing) always shows the live numbers; this page
explains them. Prices below are in US dollars; visitors in India see rupees only (the price is decided by
our server from your country, never by a switch on the page).

## When you reach an allowance

We tell you at **80%** and again at **100%** (in the app, on your billing page, by email at most twice a
week, and on WhatsApp). At 100% that action stops until you upgrade or your billing month resets; nothing
is deleted. Allowances reset on your billing date (on the 1st for Free and the trial).

Bandwidth is the one exception that never breaks a link: if your scenes use all of the month's bandwidth,
viewers keep opening them in a lighter version and you see a notice to upgrade.

## The trial

Every new account (one per person and organisation) gets 14 days with Pro features and these allowances:
5 GB storage, 10 GB bandwidth, 25,000 API calls, 3 Verse Homes, 3 films, 1 room capture, 50 renders,
1 seat and uploads up to 2 GB. The badge stays on and custom branding is not included.

**Upgrade during the trial** and the offer is applied at checkout automatically: **25% off your first
3 months** on a monthly plan, or **20% off your first year** on an annual plan. The offer ends with the trial.

After 14 days the account moves to Free. Nothing is deleted and your scenes keep opening; creating more
needs a plan.

## Broker packs

One-time, prepaid packs made for WhatsApp: **10 Verse Homes + 10 films for $12 (₹999 + 18% GST)**, or **50 + 50 for
$59 (₹4,999 + 18% GST)**. They are used after your plan's monthly allowance and never expire.

## Discount codes

Enter a code on the pricing page, or open a link with the code in it (for example
`verse.ctruh.com/pricing?code=YOURCODE`); the code is kept while you sign up. One code per purchase: a code
is not combined with the trial offer (the bigger saving applies) unless the code says it can be.

## Plans from before October 2026

If you subscribed to Pro ($79), Studio, Verse for Real Estate or All Access, nothing you pay changes. Your
subscription keeps its plan and price, and gets the new allowances wherever those are higher than what you
had. These plans are no longer sold to new customers.

## Which currency you pay in

Checkout is run by our payment partner, Dodo Payments, who adds the right sales tax, VAT or GST for your
country and issues the invoice. Customers in India pay in rupees (cards, UPI, net banking); everyone else in
US dollars.

## Changing plan

- **Upgrade** from the pricing page or the upgrade sheet; the new plan applies at once.
- **Cancel or downgrade** in **Billing**; you keep the plan until the period you paid for ends, and your scenes stay. A payment that fails has a 7-day grace period before the plan drops.
- **Refunds:** within 7 days of your first payment (or of an annual renewal). See the [refund policy](https://verse.ctruh.com/refunds), the [terms](https://verse.ctruh.com/terms) and the [privacy policy](https://verse.ctruh.com/privacy).
- **Enterprise:** request a callback on the pricing page; a specialist calls you back.
- Not sure which plan? Write to hello@ctruh.com.
